Housie

Housie for the team. One person hosts and calls numbers, and everyone else plays on their phone.

How it works

  1. The host starts a game and gets a 4-letter game code.
  2. Players open this page, type their name and the code, and get a unique ticket.
  3. The host draws numbers. Players tap numbers on their ticket to mark them.
  4. Complete a pattern, then tap Claim (or shout "Housie!"). The host checks your ticket from your name.
